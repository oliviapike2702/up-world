# Personal Development Workshop — concept & playbook

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026). Run twice by then (July 2025 at Little Louie, ~30 people, 5–8pm; October 2025 at Klaviyo, ~50 people, 3–8pm) with a Feb/March 2026 edition handed over.

## The concept

A new way of welcoming members: "join and kick-start your membership with a 100-day personal development plan". A structured plan introducing each member to Up Club's offerings in order, for ~50 new members (Marketing Manager level), repeated ~3 times a year (roughly Feb, April, September) — timed to cohort intakes.

The workshop marks the start of the 100 days. It must:

- **Build connections** — small groups, structured intros, networking moments.
- **Show vulnerability** — role-model vulnerable sharing; exercises that ask challenging questions.
- **Produce a practical PD plan** — a tangible worksheet outcome (future: uploaded to the members area, shared with their manager).
- **Tour the whole offer** — a live run-through of everything membership includes.

## Who runs it

Jo Fawcett and Sam Thomson (the exec coaches — same pair as the Accelerator) deliver; Lottie hosts; Laura helps with coaching; several of the team on the ground. Team roles on the day: timekeeping Jo/Sam hard, setting tables for group work, assigning coaches ~2 tables each, calling time on each coaching switch.

## Shape of the day (October 2025 version, 3–8pm, ~50 people)

1. Doors + mingle, Lottie icebreaker intro (~10 mins).
2. Jo + Sam segment (~1h40): career-path talk, fulfilment/growth/salary self-assessment, "who are you?" 3-words exercise, super-strengths short talk, 5-year-vision solo exercise. Break.
3. **Main "tribe" exercise (40–60 mins):** groups of 4, each person coached by their group toward "what milestones do I need to hit in the next 12 weeks" (~13 mins per person — 10 wasn't enough), coaches circulating. Then group spokespeople report back (~30 mins; keep it snappy).
4. Closing: Lottie's Up Club run-through, letter-writing to future self (letters collected in a box), wrap on a high, pizza and chatting till 8.

## Learnings so far

- 3 hours → 5 hours after feedback that people wanted longer.
- People don't move once seated for presentations — build in an opening activity that forces networking.
- Transitions (sitting into groups, coming back together) eat ~10 mins; plan for it.
- Name tags worked; pre-assigning tribe groups was great; pizza at the end was great.
- Photographer: they have plenty of shots of the same room — book for just an hour, timed for when people are moving and interacting.

## Marketing

Fills fairly naturally, doesn't need heavy pushing. Aim for ~60 sign-ups to get 50 in the room. Channels: cohort marketing email, members email, WCU mention. Reminder emails run via a HubSpot workflow; feedback via a Typeform ("Up Club Personal Development Workshop Feedback").

## Origin & scaling tension (from Drive, pulled 2026-07-22)
Born out of the June 2025 Beta cohort review — members on the "100-day" proposition asked
practical "how do I get to X" career questions, so a quarterly IRL workshop was proposed styled on
the Accelerator's "tribes" format. First ran July 2025 at ~26 attendees ("the perfect amount" per
Jo/Sam) — the October 2025 jump to ~50-60 attendees is a real, not-yet-resolved scaling tension:
more people dilutes 1:1 coaching time with just two coaches. Options considered but undecided as
of the source docs: add 2 more coaches, split into two evening sessions, or one longer day with
two groups.

**Success metric:** NPS-style "how likely to recommend," baseline 9.33/10, target lift to 9.6/10.

**Feedback themes (verbatim, Aug 2025 feedback doc):** consistent ask for more time — *"have more
time - make it a half day workshop"*, *"I would have loved for it to [be] longer - a full day
workshop"* — and more structured networking at the start/end — *"Perhaps someone to introduce you
to others on arrival,"* *"Maybe a bit of networking/chats after would have been good."* Both
directly informed the 3hr→5hr change already logged above.

**Team roles (Oct 2025 run):** Jo/Sam hosts, Lottie icebreaker host, Liv/Rachel/Abbie/Laura/Dani on
logistics, Robin on social content. Branded F&D sponsors that round: Cawston Press, Blanco Nino,
Dean's, Sipgood, Arrowtown, Funkin — consistent with the "brands volunteer product" sourcing route
below.
