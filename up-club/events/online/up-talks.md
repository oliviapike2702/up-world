# Up Talks — format & playbook

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026). Strategy-level event DNA lives in the `up-club-events` skill; this is the operational format.

**Update July 2026:** Marc no longer works on the community — the "(Marc)" tasks below need a new owner, expected Nzola (⚠ confirm).

## What they are

- The big monthly online talks with big brands: **5 per season**, all 12–1pm, on Zoom (webinar, not meeting — there's a template webinar on the hosts Zoom account).
- **Members only**, and recordings are strictly members only. Exception: a well-timed "spotlight" Up Talk each season is opened to all when doors are opening — it's a proven conversion driver (e.g. the Bold Bean open talk in Sept 2025).
- Purpose: build credibility with sexy brands, toward the goal of being *the* go-to place for start-up marketing development.
- Planning horizon: a full season of Up Talks booked before it starts (e.g. Jan–March locked by November); book speakers **the moment they say yes** — a confirmed date, title and calendar invite immediately, or it drags.

## What makes a good speaker

- Really interesting careers; people who were at a cool company during growth; brands who are killer at one specific thing (Bold Bean at events, Thursday at partnerships); genuine marketing experts (e.g. psychology in marketing).
- Past speakers include Lush, Ryanair, Monzo, Bold Bean, Thursday, Oddbox, BrewDog, Puresport, Grind, Botivo, Damson Madder.
- Finding topics: ask the community what they want, brief Lottie to open doors to speakers, poll the community if a speaker has several ideas.

## Hosting

- **James Hickson is the killer fireside host.** At the start of a season, send him the dates/speakers and ask which he wants; call out for hosts (Jonny, Laura H have done it) for the rest.
- Host logs into the hosts Zoom account. Get them to rename themselves "James at Up World" rather than "Host".

## Initial setup checklist (per talk)

1. Collect from speaker: date, topic, 3 bullet points, headshot, bio, fireside vs presentation (agree format early — fireside doesn't suit every topic).
2. Google Calendar invite on the event calendar, plus 15 min tech-check beforehand (Marc).
3. Luma event + event bio (Marc), graphic from the Canva templates (Marc), add to the website events page (Marc; Alfie has the login).
4. Create the Zoom webinar from the template (Marc).

## Run-up checklist (per talk)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 10 days out | Reach out to speaker: confirm style, share sign-up numbers, get their WhatsApp |
| 1 week out | Members email + Slack #mymembership post (tag @channel); make a WhatsApp group (host + speaker + you) and have the host call the speaker |
| 5 days out | One-pager for the host (questions, bio, format) |
| 2 days out | Check speaker is listed as webinar panellist and has their unique join link (resend + WhatsApp it) |
| 1 day out | Give the host the Zoom login |
| On the day | Join the 15-min prep slot to check tech, then rejoin as a guest; assign a Brand Hacker as resident hype-man in the chat; make sure the host holds "host" duties so the Zoom doesn't die when you leave |
| After | Thank the speaker, get their address, send a gift (Marc); share feedback when it lands |

Common day-of issues: speaker loses their unique join link (resend it), host forgets the Zoom login.

## After the talk

Recording to the Training Hub in the members area. Kasturi turned talks into public blogs and Robin clipped them for socials — process was "not 10/10 yet" and owners have changed; the ambition (showcase the insights on LinkedIn/email) still stands.
