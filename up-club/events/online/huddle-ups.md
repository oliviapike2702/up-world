# Huddle Ups — format & playbook

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026).

## What they are

Intimate, focused online discussions on one specific topic, **capped at 8 members**, roughly 2 a month. A Huddle Up is *not* a talk, a workshop, a lecture, or a place to sell.

The bar: content you couldn't google. Super specific, targeted at genuine pain points, full of insights — this is core to the ambition of Up Club being the go-to place for start-up marketing development.

## Structure (1 hour)

- 0–10 mins: intros
- 10–35 mins: insights
- 35–55 mins: Q&A
- 55–60 mins: wrap up

## Scheduling and hosts

- Dream state: **2 months of Huddle Ups scheduled ahead** (leave a couple of gaps for reactive topics).
- Brand Hackers should host 2 slots a month — pod leads nominate 2 people each month and need chasing.
- Freelancers love hosting because it wins them work, and that's fine — but it is **not a place to sell**; hosts have been told off for it before.
- Topic sourcing: poll the community when someone has multiple ideas; mine popular #askthecommunity threads.

## Reporting

One metric as of Jan 2026: **new Huddle Up attendees per week** (new = never attended a Huddle Up before, not new member), via a Zapier feeding a HubSpot segment.
