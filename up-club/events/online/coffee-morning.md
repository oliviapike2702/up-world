# Coffee Morning — format

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026).

## What it is

Weekly members' coffee morning: **every Wednesday, 10–10:30am, Zoom**. Aim: marketers start their day energised and leave feeling connected to members they could now call up with a problem. Dream outcome: a handful of in-house marketers from hot brands solve someone's live work problem together, then form their own Slack group chat.

## KPI and time-box

- Send ~20 invites a week; get **5 in-house marketers** attending.
- Whole process ≤ 45 mins/week (30 on the call, 15 inviting via Slack DMs to lurkers). It's a nice-to-have, not a project — unless someone can prove it's a major retention/acquisition lever.

## How it runs

Recurring Luma invite + recurring Zoom link + automated Slack #mymembership message (Zapier). Weekly stats go into the reporting sheet. Jot down member insights wherever they come up.

## Insights (Jan 2026)

- Mostly freelancers show up (free at that hour, often lonelier) — fine, but the goal is in-house attendance.
- Lots of no-shows.
- A few die-hard regulars.
- No structure so far; possibly something to design in future.
