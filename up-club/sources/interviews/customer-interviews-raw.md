# Up Club — raw customer interview quotes

Source: "Member Calls Sep 2025," "Customer Insights - onboarding calls," "Onboarding Call
Reflections," "Customer interviews - Kasturi" (Google Drive), pulled 2026-07-22. Raw material —
never edited, only pulled from into `../../customers/` or elsewhere. Theme analysis pulled from
these quotes lives in `../../customers/customer-insights.md`, not here.

## Verbatim quotes

**Harriet Cox** (freelancer)
> A few different people that I've spoken to have talked about Up… So I thought 2025 is the year
> that I should give something a go that's different.

**Graham Moffat** (agency)
> Really need to get some leads… if we can get some leads from it, I don't grudge the cost.

> I actually didn't realize there were so many conversations going on, but it's actually been
> fairly quiet.

**Georgina** (fractional CMO, ex-Copy Club member)
> If we provide the structure, freelancers would love it.

Also flagged wanting more B2B-specific Huddle Ups and "Ask me anything" takeovers from senior
guest experts.

**Ceylin**
Described Up Club as "like the earlier days of LinkedIn" — the most "curated" community she's
tried, valued for finding freelance clients even after shifting out of a marketing role. Also
wished there'd been an icebreaker activity at a Christmas meetup, since people stayed siloed in
existing groups.

**Taylor Churchman**
Joined after overhearing his manager mention a "Copy Club 10am coffee meeting" — membership has
always been company-subsidised for him. Values being able to "watch a webinar... or get backup
from a human opinion." Receives too many emails so "either skims, marks as read or deletes,"
dislikes overly technical/niche content.

**Onboarding call pattern** (per "Customer Insights - onboarding calls")

**Small-team/solo pattern** (per "Onboarding Call Reflections")
