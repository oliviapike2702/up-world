## What the June names actually say

1. **Renewal points are where churn happens.** 9 of 22 cancelled at an exact renewal boundary:
   Romy, Jonjo and Julia at the 12-month annual mark, and the whole Sep-2025 In House Quarterly
   cohort (Hannah O'Donnell, Jess Hughes, Alice Loveday, Chloe O'Driscoll) at 9 months, plus
   Charles Boustany at 3. Renewal-week outreach would hit most of June's churn before it happens.
2. **The legacy £20 monthly cohort is quietly draining.** 8 of 22 were on the 2022 £20 plan with
   20 to 30 months' tenure. Helen Tarver named the price increase directly. These are long-tenure
   members lapsing out, not unhappy ones; different treatment from new-joiner churn.
3. **"No time" remains the biggest preventable bucket** (8), which is exactly the segment Nzola's
   strategy doc targets ("they don't know how to get value from the product").
4. **Win-backs work and are the KR margin.** Q2 lands at 74 net vs 76 gross. Without Jonjo and
   Hannah Cooke the quarter misses the target. Jonjo's case (boss cancelled, member unaware) also
   suggests checking who actually requested company-paid cancellations before treating them as decisions.
5. **Redundancy cluster forming.** 3 June leavers changed role or were let go; the first two July
   cancellations (Hibah Rehman, Emily Freeman) are the same story. Worth watching in July.
