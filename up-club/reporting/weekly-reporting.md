# Weekly reporting — the metrics framework

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026). Happens **every Monday**; numbers go into the "Up Club Reporting" Google Sheet (Looms/explanations sit under each segment in the sheet). Monthly churn analysis is separate → `README.md`.

## Topline

Weekly: number of members (Memberspace) · churn % (Stripe) · number of cancelled members.
Monthly: monthly/quarterly/annual split (Memberspace) · brand/freelancer/agency split (Memberspace) — guards the 80/15/5 mix target.

## Top of funnel

Weekly: website traffic (GA4) · Up World LinkedIn (posts, impressions, UTM clicks) · Liv LinkedIn (same) · newsletter stats · social stats.

## Member engagement

Weekly: new members attending Huddle Ups (HubSpot segment fed by Zapier — "new" = first-ever Huddle Up) · WCU email OR %, CTR %, unsub % (Mailchimp) · weekly active users on Slack % (Slack admin) · % logged into members area (Memberspace).
