# Referrals — the unsolved growth channel

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026).

## Why it matters

**~50% of new members come from members telling their mates.** Members who love it genuinely rave about it. The open challenge: get people to shout even more, and make sure **in-house members refer other in-house members** (freelancers happily refer freelancers because they can win work — but that's not the target customer and threatens the 80/15/5 mix).

## Why it's hard

- Many members joined precisely because they have no marketing mates — so they have no one to tell.
- Money-off/free-membership incentives don't promote retention, and most members' companies pay anyway, so discounts aren't that attractive.
- Whatever we do has to stay cheap.

## What's been tried

- Personalised thank-yous.
- Tiered prizes: 1 referral = cookie, 2 = cake, 10+ = Fortnum & Mason hamper.
- Refer 2 people → we pay for a coaching session.
- Thank-you cookies (the **current** process — runs after each cohort, brief: "Up Club Referral Brief Cookie"; Marc ran it until he left community work in July 2026 — ⚠ new owner needed).
- Formal ambassador programmes were investigated and rejected.

Lottie's observation: the one time people actually acted was scarcity + immediacy — at 999 members, "the 1,000th member gets a box of donuts delivered, here and now".

Still unsolved as of Jan 2026. Treat any new referral idea as an experiment against this history.
