Up Club insights and positioning 

 - Marketing Leaders 

The challenge: 
 - Make it irresistibly valuable to marketing leaders so they desperately want to get their teams signed up. 
 - Make it as easy as humanly possible for them to get sign-off from budget holders. 

Summary and implications of key findings: 

The current perception of Up Club's is too far towards the ‘vibey community for marketeers’. While leaders see the benefits of joining themselves in terms of network and recommendations, it lacks tangible, measurable benefits that will a) persuade them to sign their teams up and b) are a ‘must have’ for them to get sign off from budget holders in start-ups where the budget is not already allocated.

To achieve this, Up Club should aim to reposition themselves as a platform that delivers tangible and measurable training (i.e. marketing for start ups - how to build a brand course) with specific outcomes and ROI from membership.   To differentiate from other courses and qualifications, Up Club should lean into their existing strengths i.e:
	•	Up Club is the only course designed specifically for start-up marketers:
 - It covers everything you’d get working at a blue-chip, but we understand the realities of working in a start up too, where executing can look very different. 
 - You get on-going access to a network of current marketing experts from leading brands such as x, y and z to help you solve problems fast, and make impactful partnerships. 
 - In a world where approaches and techniques change quickly, our material stays up to date by its nature - it's brought to you by industry leading start-ups. 
	•	Mentorship from current industry experts from game-changing brands. 
	•	Value for money vs. one off courses. 

 Drivers of appeal - Insights from marketing leaders on the value they currently see for their teams 

There are two key value propositions emerging from the insights from current marketing leader members. However, it is important to remember that these are mostly views from people already using the platform, and these benefits are not necessarily understood by non-members looking in. 

1. Amplifying impact A performance multiplier - helping teams be more effective and efficient through:

	•	Making faster, better (more informed) decisions 
	•	Making powerful connections (brand partnerships, experts etc.) at speed which drive growth 
	•	Connecting with trusted freelancers and agencies, faster 
	•	Staying up to date with industry trends 
	•	Expanding thinking and improving problem solving - getting out of the silo of their own team and getting inspo from experts

2. A team development shortcut - helping leaders improve team performance without the hours of 121 training:

	•	Up Club gives your team access to mentors to take the strain of team development and support growth 
	•	Training from game-changing marketing leaders which inspires and evolves your teams skills and thinking

2. Marketing Managers

Context:

The challenge: 
 - Make it irresistibly valuable to marketing managers so they make the case to their leaders to pay for their subscription. 
 - Equip them with what they need to make a case to their managers.
