- **Brand Manager Bootcamp (BMBC)** — course, Sept 2026 cohort, early-bird slots. Sign up direct (no application needed).
- **Up Talks** — monthly online talks with brands killing it. 1 hour, Zoom, ~50 attendees, presentation or fireside. Speakers sourced via outreach.
- **Mentoring programme** — members paired with mentors.
- **Accelerator** — with James McAulay (content hosted on Thinkific).
