**Source:** page 8 of Chay's architecture diagrams, [up-club-hubspot-diagrams-15jul2026.pdf](../reporting/up-club-hubspot-diagrams-15jul2026.pdf)

Not in the PRD — these are revenue/ops opportunities that came out of having clean data
for the first time, not architecture.

- **Leak ledger:** ~£20k/yr in paused/voided billing, now visible with an owner per case.
- **Win-back list:** 2,771 catalogued ex-members with logged leave reasons (~£15-20k/yr
  at 3% reactivation).
- **Corporate seats:** already happening ad hoc (InMotion, Saputo, gupuds, Vypr, Storal) —
  could package as a formal B2B product. Relates to [agency-memberships.md](agency-memberships.md).
- **"Big Up escalator":** 90 members, zero churn, health data could double as an
  upgrade-qualification list for Big Up Club. Relates to [proposition.md](proposition.md).
