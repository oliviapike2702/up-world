## Member mix — a deliberate target, not an accident

The community is held at roughly **80% in house / 15% freelancer / 5% agency**, tracked weekly.

Why it matters: in-house members don't want to be in a community full of freelancers, and freelancers want a space that is predominantly in-house (that's where their clients are). Losing this proportion damages the proposition for everyone, so growth activity (referrals, cohorts, agency sales) is always weighted toward in-house members.
