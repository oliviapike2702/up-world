# Up Club — proposition

Source: Google Doc "Up Club Proposition March 2026," pulled from Drive 2026-07-22. This is a live
working doc for a proposition rewrite, not a finished/approved proposition — treat headline
options as candidates, not confirmed copy.

Old draft (kept for reference, thinner than the below): "where ambitious start-up marketers learn
to make their work more impactful, from people who are killing it." ~1,200 members.

## Context driving the rewrite
Moved to cohorts last year to create urgency to join. Still struggling to hit 100 people per
cohort (want 200). A year of testing and learning; coming back to the proposition to make sure the
messaging on what's being sold is uber clear.

## Diagnosis of the current page (up-world.co/for-marketers)
Does a good job explaining *what* Up Club is, but doesn't make the problem feel urgent enough, the
outcome concrete enough, or the next step easy enough.

**Strengths:** the product itself is good; plenty of social proof; on-brand style.

**Weaknesses:**
- Problem too vague — "Learn faster, connect smarter, and level up your marketing career" doesn't
  name the specific pains (solo marketer, unsure if what I'm doing is right, need faster answers,
  need to prove impact to my boss)
- Value feels like a feature list, not outcomes — e.g. "Access 100s of talks from top marketing
  experts" should become "Steal the exact playbooks other startups are using right now"
- No section for members needing manager sign-off, despite knowing they want it
- CTA creates friction — removes urgency since doors don't open until May, people feel they can
  come back later
- Page too long — the core "why join" answer is too far down, causing scroll fatigue
- Transformation isn't clear — good at showing what people get, not what's different after

**3 questions to get uber clear on:** what do members actually brag about after joining; what do
managers notice first; what problem makes people join fastest?

## Proposed structure
Problem → Relevance → Solution → Proof → Logistics → Action

## Candidate headline propositions
1. **"Stop running marketing on instinct alone, start making decisions backed by experience"** —
   within 3 months, faster/more confident decisions on channels, budgets, priorities, because
   others have solved the same problems before
2. **"Move from reactive marketing to controlled growth"** — within 3 months, less firefighting,
   more planning/executing/improving campaigns that drive steady progress
3. **"Stop wasting budget on campaigns that don't work. Fix that before you start Q3"** — you don't
   need more budget, you need better decisions; within 3 months, spot underperformance sooner,
   redirect spend, invest in what delivers results
- Repeated tagline candidate across the page: "You don't need more budget. You need to make better
  decisions."
- CTA candidate: "Join the waitlist to get early access to March & April's Up Talks."

## The problem (draft copy)
"You're expected to deliver growth, but often make big decisions with limited experience and
resources." / "You're responsible for results, but don't have all the facts to validate what's
working and what's wasting budget." / "You're moving fast, but get stuck solving problems that
don't move the needle." / "You're doing the work and trying to make your impact visible when
there's so much else going on in the business."

## The promise (draft copy)
Stop guessing, start deciding with confidence · fix problems earlier, not after results drop ·
stop working in isolation · show results leadership can see. (References a Lottie Woolley case
study as proof.)

## What members get (how it works)
Personal Development Workshop with leading coaches · weekly expert-led discussions · 1:1 expert
mentorship · monthly live talks from leading brands/leaders · a private Slack community · an
always-on support network.
