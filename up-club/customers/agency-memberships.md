# Up Club — agency memberships

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026).

## Positioning

- £3,000/year inc VAT (see `offer-pricing.md`). The most expensive tier, so it needs a **longer lead time** and a more considered sell.
- Agencies are capped at **~5% of the community**, and they need to be the "right" agencies. Direction of travel: tighten the proposition so agencies are treated more like partners/sponsors of the community than ordinary members.
- Evidence the demand is there: two agency memberships once sold off a single Lottie LinkedIn post that generated 80+ DMs (though most were cold).

## Sales process (as of Jan 2026)

1. Agency applies via the website → posted in Slack and added to the Deals board in HubSpot.
2. **Abbie holds the first call** — a "selling" call framed as an application we're reviewing (keeps it exclusive).
3. **Liv holds a second vetting call** with more membership detail. (During Liv's 2026 sabbatical this was collapsed to one call with Abbie deciding — the two-call shape is the default.)
4. Liv sends a congratulations email with the agency Memberspace payment link.
5. On payment they auto-enrol into the HubSpot **agency onboarding workflow** (flagged as "a bit scrappy" — improvement candidate).

## Watch-outs

- The application framing matters: it should feel like they're being accepted, not buying.
- Freelancers/agencies must never use the community to sell — the value exchange is generosity first (see `../context/onboarding-and-intros.md` for how this is coached in onboarding).
