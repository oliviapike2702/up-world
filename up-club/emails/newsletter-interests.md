# Newsletter interests (for the morning digest)

Draft, built from what lands in Liv's inbox and what she forwards to the team. Liv to correct.

## What Liv cares about (surface these)
The test: could Liv use it for Up Club? She forwards newsletter items to the team with ideas ("member prompt of the week in the WCU email", "great idea for the test", "interesting thoughts on newsletter structure"). So prioritise things she can action.

- **AI tools and news relevant to marketers** — new models, practical AI use cases, prompts, tools members would care about. (Sources seen: There's An AI For That, Lenny's / How I AI, The Workbench, Bristol AI Shed.)
- **Growth / email / CRM / marketing tactics** — anything on acquisition, retention, email & SMS, content. (SYSTM / Matt Lerner, Beyond the Inbox, Content Playbook.)
- **Community building** — running communities, events, member engagement. (London Community Week, communityled.world, The Hearth.)
- **Content ideas she can repurpose** for the WCU email or Up Talks.
- **Jobs / opportunities** relevant to startup marketers. (Generalist World.)
- **Events worth attending** for her role (community, marketing, AI meetups).

## What to ignore (do not surface)
- Retail / personal shopping promos: Anthropologie, Mountain Warehouse, Sisters & Seekers, etc.
- Restaurant / food marketing and supper clubs (unless it's a genuine work event).
- Generic product upsells and "upgrade to Pro" nudges.
- Anything addressed to personal or unrelated inboxes that isn't work-relevant.

## Digest format (proposed)
- One Slack message to Liv each morning.
- Group by theme (AI, Growth/Marketing, Community, Jobs, Events).
- Per item: one line on what it is + why it might matter + link.
- Keep it skimmable. Only genuinely relevant items, quality over quantity. If a quiet day, say so rather than padding.
