## The season at a glance

Every startup marketer is being told to use AI. Almost none are being told how to use it well, and most are doing it alone. Up Club is where they figure it out together. The season runs July to the first week of October, when the doors open.

**The pieces:**
- **Lottie's manifesto** ("Our approach to AI") — the anchor everything points back to
- **Monthly AI Up Talks** — members-only speaker sessions
- **Weekly AI Huddle Ups** — already running, AI at the heart
- **AI wins and fails Slack channel** — already built, straight off the poll (top vote)
- **6-week Claude Challenge** — build something together over six weeks (still being shaped, see Post 4)
- **IRL Claude Create Day** — half-day, September, build in small groups
- **A paid AI course** — coming, the longer-term revenue play and funnel

### What the community actually said

**The Slack poll (votes):** AI wins/fails channel 18, Monthly AI Up Talks 16, 6-week Claude Challenge 16, AI Huddle Up series 10, paid AI course 6, IRL Hackathon 6.

**The survey (around 18 responses):** this isn't an AI-curious crowd, it's AI-active. Roughly 15 of 18 already use it regularly or say it's core to how they work. What they want is the next level: building workflows and automations and agents, getting output that sounds like them not generic, and knowing which tools are worth it. On how they learn, two pulls dominate: live with a speaker they can question, and building alongside other people with some accountability. There's a small, honest beginner voice too ("I NEED beginners everything haha").

**Internal, worth knowing:** the paid course came joint-last on the poll and mid-table in the survey. People want the free, communal, build-together stuff most. Don't lead the public campaign with the course; let it ride underneath.

**Leads the survey threw off (for later, not public yet):** a member who does voice-to-copybot work offered to help run something; another recommended Sean O'Dwyer at Fabled Labs. Ideas worth stealing: AI use-case sprints, and a members-only AI tool built on Up Club's own content.

### The story spine

Lottie's manifesto is the spine. There's a clear choice for marketers: lean into AI or get left behind. The differentiator isn't technical skill, it's mindset, enthusiasm and openness. Leaning in means finding time to play, and honestly that's outside the 9 to 5. Even Lottie feels the overwhelm and knows she's still wasting time not being best in class. Up Club is the place you do this with other people instead of alone. Every post points back to that.

## Voice, quickly

The usual guardrails: UK English, no em dashes, no emojis unless Lottie uses one first, opinionated hook, short sentences, one story per post, any ask last and brief. Banned: delve, leverage, unlock, superpower, "it's worth noting", "here's the thing".
