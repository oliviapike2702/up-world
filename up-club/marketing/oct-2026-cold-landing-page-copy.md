## 3. What it actually is

## So what is it, plainly

A paid membership for startup marketers. It runs in cohorts, and the next one opens on 5 October.

- **A private Slack group.** Post a question in the morning and get answers from marketers who've
  already solved it.
- **A mentor of your own**, matched to you, one to one.
- **Monthly talks with marketers from brands you know.** OceanSaver, DASH, Botivo, Grind, Estrid.
  (We call them Up Talks.)
- **Small sessions on one problem at a time.** Eight people, one topic. Fixing a welcome flow that
  isn't driving first sales, getting out of TikTok view jail, setting up SEO properly when you're a
  startup. (We call them Huddle Ups.)
- **A workshop to start you off**, run by two marketing coaches: Johanna Fawcett (ex Zizzi, Côte,
  P&G) and Lou Nylander (fractional CMO & coach). Both names hyperlinked to LinkedIn.
- **Templates and recordings** you can take and use straight away.

> This section does not exist on the current page and it is the single biggest cold-traffic gap.
> Barrier #1 in the positioning research is "uncertainty over what Up Club actually is".
> The "(We call them Up Talks)" device glosses insider words while teaching them. Keep it.

## The pop-up

Exit intent on desktop. 50% scroll plus a time floor on mobile (exit intent does not exist on
touch, and most paid traffic is mobile). Once per visitor, dismissal remembered. Never on load.

### Before you go, fancy a free year?

We're giving away one annual membership, worth £300. Winner drawn on
`[date — set it a few days before 5 October]`.

**[ email ]**  **[ which best describes you? ]**

**[ Enter me ]**

`[collapsible terms]`

> **Draw timing matters.** Set it just before 5 Oct so the "you didn't win" email doubles as the
> doors-open email. Otherwise the draw is a dead end and the first thing a cold lead hears from you
> is that they lost.
>
> **Terms are a legal requirement, not a nicety.** CAP Code needs, stated before entry: how to
> enter and who's eligible, closing date, the prize described exactly, how and when the winner is
> picked and notified, and the promoter's name and address. Meta also require a line saying they
> are not associated with the promotion.
>
> **Define the prize precisely.** Tiers are In House £300, Freelancer £600, Agency £3,000. Write it
> as "one In House annual membership, worth £300" or you're exposed if a freelancer wins.

## Rules this copy follows

- No em dashes. UK English. Sentence-case headings. No arrows in button text.
- No member counts anywhere (Liv's instruction, 6 Aug). Also unresolvable: HubSpot says 845, the
  site says both "1,000+" and "1,078", the TOV doc says "~1,200".
- Feelings are evoked through a scene, never named. "It's not that you lack confidence" got 29.3%,
  the worst non-logistics open rate of the last 45 sends.
- No unverified stats. The live page's "92% increased revenue" and "7 months to promotion" are
  **not used here** — no source exists for either in the workspace.
- Insider vocabulary always glossed on first use.
