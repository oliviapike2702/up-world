# Up Club AI Season
## July – September 2026

---

### The opportunity

Every startup marketer right now is being told to use AI. Almost none of them are being told how to use it well. They're getting generic output, wasting time on the wrong tools, and quietly falling behind. Up Club is the place to fix that — and the window to own this space is now, before it becomes crowded.

This document sets out what we're building, why, and what we need to get there.

---

## 1. How we talk about AI

This is the foundation. Before anything gets launched or marketed, we need a clear point of view — or the whole season is just a pile of events.

**Lottie's manifesto (plain text)**
Lottie's written take on AI in startup marketing: what she believes, what she's doing, where she draws the line. Written for Up Club's audience specifically — not agencies, not corporates. Startup marketers who are time-poor, under-resourced, and being expected to figure this out alone. This is the anchor piece. It comes first, and everything else points back to it.

**Lottie's talk: "Don't sleep on AI"**
The live version of the manifesto. Open to members and non-members. Planned for July. Works as a quick win and as acquisition for the September cohort — people who come for the talk, stay for the community.

**Building out loud on LinkedIn**
Lottie (and Liv) documenting what we're building in real time — what's working, what isn't, what we're learning. Not polished content. Raw, specific, reactive. This is the ongoing marketing engine for the season and the September opening.

**Feedback loops**
Member surveys, Slack polls, responding publicly to what the community says. Baked into everything we do, not bolted on at the end. Keeps us honest and gives us content.

### Notes on the initiatives

**Survey first, always.** The course structure and the AI Up Talks speaker list both depend on what comes back from the research. Don't commit to either until you have responses.

**Up Talks vs. course — keep these distinct.** Monthly AI Up Talks = free taster, product driver, community value. Paid AI Course = structured deep dive, revenue driver, external audience. Different jobs. If this distinction blurs, both suffer.

**The hackathon is not a revenue line.** £15 to charity means the value is in the community moment and the September acquisition story, not the income. Worth being clear so it doesn't get miscounted.

**James McAulay** is an obvious anchor for the paid course — his May talk showed there's appetite and he already understands the Up Club audience. Worth a direct conversation about a structured programme.

**Chay** — great fit for the Claude Challenge alongside Nzola on community management. Needs confirming.
