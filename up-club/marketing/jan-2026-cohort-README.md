# Up Club Jan 2026 cohort (doors opening) — campaign record

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Liv Handover doc (Jan 2026). The membership doors-opening playbook and results, written as Liv handed over before sabbatical. Up Club opens doors ~3 times a year (Jan, May, Sept).

## Target and result

- Target: 100 new members. September 2025 comparison: ended doors week on 81, hustled to a **round 90**.
- Jan 2026 felt harder than Jan 2025, when minimal marketing hit nearly 100 — Liv's own flag: "are we trying to solve the wrong problem?" and "100 new members 3 times a year shouldn't be this hard".

## Funnel benchmarks (Jan 2026 vs Sept 2025)

- Paid waitlist slots converted: **73%** (vs 80% Sept).
- Unpaid waitlist converted: **12%** (vs 20% Sept), and the unpaid pool was much smaller (7 joiners vs 20).
- Only **10% of new members were genuinely new contacts** (vs ~40% in Sept 2025 and April) — a sign boosted posts/ads brought in fewer fresh people than assumed.
- Day-one sign-ups: 44 (vs 30 in Sept) but Thursday/Friday went much quieter than usual.
- GA4 doors-week traffic: 528 (vs 727 in Sept 2025).

## Growth tactics run

1. **Lottie lead-gen talk** ("Why your marketing isn't driving growth", open to all): 206 sign-ups, 118 attended; 84 non-member sign-ups, 43 attended; ~10% of non-members converted (expectation is ~20%; the Sept Bold Bean talk likely did better). Learning: Lottie's energy sells, but the talk topic needs to be killer.
2. **Instantly** (cold outreach inbox).
3. **LinkedUp Challenge** (30-day LinkedIn posting challenge for members).
4. **Liv on Expandi** (LinkedIn automation): connection rates fine, reply rate typically 5–10%; campaigns from Lottie's account get 10–20 points higher connection rates.

Experiment: waitlist changed to **deposit-only** ("you can't just join the waitlist") — part of why the unpaid pool shrank.

## Waitlist machinery (reused each cohort)

- Per-cohort spreadsheet + HubSpot workflow + Typeform + website switch-over + waitlist segments + nurture flow (mapped in Miro).
- **Warm leads** segment = anyone who clicked previous doors-open campaigns or joined a previous waitlist (HubSpot segment "Up Club Warm Leads").
- Pre-doors warm-lead email arc (Jan 2026): "What life could look like" → "This event changed my life" → "DON'T PAY FOR YOUR MEMBERSHIP" (companies pay angle) → Huddle Up line-up.
- Waitlisters are excluded from general comms during launch periods.

## Learnings for the next cohort

- Fresh top-of-funnel is the real constraint — conversion of the existing warm pool is fine; the "new contacts" share is what collapsed.
- A killer open-to-all talk is the single best converter; book a big-name spotlight talk for doors week.
- Don't assume boosting/ads replace genuinely new audiences.
