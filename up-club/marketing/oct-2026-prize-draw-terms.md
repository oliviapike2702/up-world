## Things a lawyer should look at

1. **Clause 8 is the one that matters.** The draw's commercial purpose is list building, so the
   terms say plainly that entrants get added to marketing. Under UK GDPR and PECR, whether that is
   sufficient (rather than needing a separate unticked consent box on the form) depends on how the
   HubSpot form is built. If the form already has its own consent field, this clause should match
   it rather than contradict it.
2. **Trading name.** The page says Up Club and Up World throughout; the promoter is The Copy Club
   Limited. That is fine, but the trading-name relationship should be stated somewhere a reader can
   find it.
3. **The £300 prize value** should be checked against what an In House annual membership actually
   costs on the day the draw closes, not on the day this was written.
