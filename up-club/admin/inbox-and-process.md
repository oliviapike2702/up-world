## The SayHi inbox

- The SayHi email is where membership queries, match-making leads and general questions land. It's hosted in **HubSpot under Conversations > Inbox**; each email arrives as a ticket.
- Mission framing: every customer touch point should be incredible. The inbox is an extension of the team and must reflect the tone of voice (see `../../context/tov/Up Club tone of voice.md` — "your infectious mate").
- Every time someone has to write in, it slightly damages their experience (especially new members) — so speed matters.

## Cadence and hygiene

- Check **twice a day**, morning and afternoon. Linking the inbox to your own email means tickets land straight in your inbox.
- Reply yourself in HubSpot, or tag the right owner:
  - Up World (if you can't answer) → Marc / Abbie
  - Big Up Club → Abbie
  - Brand Hackers → Will
  - Recruitment (Up Talent) → Laura
- Close every ticket once sorted. Keep the inbox as close to zero as possible.
- Stuck → ask Marc.

## Common queries

Most frequent: pause or cancel a membership, help getting into Slack, updating payment details, switching a membership to someone else on their team.

- Template answers live in the Google Doc "Community Emails / Questions Template Responses".
- Loom how-tos exist for: pausing memberships, cancelling memberships, making a coupon (links in the Jan 2026 handover doc).

## Cancellations

- Process is run via a Monday board (Loom "How to cancel with Monday Board" in the handover doc); Marc knows it inside out.
- Every leaver goes into the "Up World Leaver Feedback" sheet — this feeds the monthly churn analysis in `../reporting/`.
- Known problem areas (as of Jan 2026): the process isn't automated, and leaver feedback collection is patchy.
