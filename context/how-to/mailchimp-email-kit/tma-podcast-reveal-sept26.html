Hey gang,

I sat down with Charlotte Langley, CMO at Bloom & Wild, to record this module's episode on building a brand with a tight budget.

We both agreed that there's no point spending £50k on brand if ultimately you still think what you're trying to do is drive short-term sales. Most businesses fail to separate the two. They spend on brand, then judge it by whether it converted this week, and call it a failure when it doesn't.

We also had a big chat about how to maintain consistency. Bloom & Wild's charity partner is Carers Trust. Their whole brand is built on care, so a partner that reinforces that does real work for them. Donating to, say, the World Wildlife Fund wouldn't, however good the cause, because it doesn't say anything about who they are.

Every choice you make as a brand either reinforces the brand or dilutes it, and most businesses never think that hard about which.

This episode was filmed to go alongside Module 2 of The Marketing Accelerator where we learn all about Brand Building, including:

  • Different brand building objectives
  • Aligning on your brand identity
