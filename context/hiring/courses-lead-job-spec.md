# Courses Lead

Own the product and growth of the Up World course programme which we built to help get startup
marketers promoted.

[SALARY PLACEHOLDER]. Full time. Remote first, London based. You'll need to be available for team
meetings and events twice a month IRL.

## The short version

You'll take on 2 flagship courses which we run, [Brand Manager Boot Camp](https://www.up-world.co/brand-manager-bootcamp)
and [The Marketing Accelerator](https://www.up-world.co/the-marketing-accelerator). These both run
twice a year and produce really great results. But they're not close to as big or as good as it could
be, and we want someone to take that personally! At the moment we have around 80 people in a year
take these courses and they bring in about £100k a year. We think we can easily double this. The only
reason it hasn't happened yet is because we've never had someone to solely focus on it.

Up World is four people, so there's no approval chain to get through. Have an idea on a Monday and you
could be running it inside the month, and Lottie will back a good one fast. You'll have room to change
things, including the things we like. Bring us something we haven't thought of, even if it makes us a
bit nervous.

You'll also decide how much of this job a person needs to do at all. A team this small can only run
something this big by automating hard, and that's a big part of the job here.

## Up World

Up World is the home for ambitious startup marketers. Almost 10 years ago, Lottie organised a supper
club and a community grew out of it. We are a small but mighty team and we do a lot.

- Up Club is the heart of everything. Members get access to people ahead of them in their careers,
  training, mentoring, weekly sessions where members bring a real problem to the group, talks from
  marketers doing the job well, dinners, and a Slack they can message in a crisis on a Tuesday
  afternoon.
- Big Up Club is an invite-only community designed for start-up Marketing Directors, Heads of
  Marketing, and CMOs to learn, grow, and thrive from the best UK marketing minds. We launched the
  community last February to a very selective group and have managed new members carefully since then.
  We currently have 70 members.
- Our courses are how people get promotions. Brand Manager Boot Camp and The Marketing Accelerator
  both run twice a year. Both get great feedback and they produce incredible results every time. This
  is yours to run!

We're really ambitious. Our vision is a service so useful our members can't do their jobs without it.
We're not saving the world, but we've seen what this does for people. Without us, a lot of them have
no structured development, can't reach their potential, and find professional life very lonely.

The team:

- Currently recruiting full time Head of Up World
- **Abbie,** Membership & Partnership manager - owns Big Up Club / Sponsorship
- **Nzola,** Community Executive - owns the day to day running of Up Club.
- **Lottie** - Founder / Strategy partner. She's not involved in day to day decisions but acts as
  strategic sounding board
- You!

Liv, who does this role today is moving into a different role in the business so will be there for
support.

## The role

You own courses. The strategy, the products, the revenue, and how good they are.

We're not asking you to arrive and tear up something that works, and we're not asking you to leave it
alone either. We want someone who understands why it works, then makes it something people can't
imagine not paying for.

Two things make ours different from every other marketing course, and both are yours to protect and
push. The first is the coaching. Real coaches, working with people on their own careers, not just
teaching. The second is the graduation dinner, which is where people realise they've actually changed
something. Those are the reason people recommend us, and they're the hardest part to copy.

What exists and works:

- The Marketing Accelerator - our marketing leadership course for marketers looking for a promotion
  to head of.
- Brand Manager Boot Camp - teaches the marketing fundamentals for junior marketers looking to become
  brand managers.
- A course playbook we trust. You're not arriving to reinvent how a cohort runs. We know that bit.
- Three coaches who are genuinely great, and more in the pipeline.

What doesn't exist yet:

- We're dreaming of an AI course to kick off at the start of 2027 - we want to be the people startup
  marketers come to for that rather than the people catching up… **this is for you to create!!**
- Short, specific courses. We think there's something in running focused mini-courses on CRM, paid
  ads and social with Brand Hackers, our sister agency. It's an idea rather than a plan, and we'd
  love someone to go and prove it.
- Up Academy. If all of that works, courses stop being a thing Up World does and become a business in
  their own right. We're not going to pretend that's a promise. It's where this could go, and you'd be
  the person taking it there.

You'll report to our Head of Up World. They set the direction for Up World as a whole and hold the
commercial picture. **Inside courses, the direction is yours.**

## What you'll own

**The number**

- £100k of course revenue today, and the job of doubling it. Cohorts currently run at 20 to 25
  people. We think 40 is possible.
- Pricing, and the argument for changing it.
- The funnel. Launch campaigns, warm leads, paid, the landing pages, the follow-up. You'll brief our
  freelancers and hold the quality, and you'll write the thing yourself when it's faster.
- Knowing why people don't buy, in specifics, not in theory.

**The products**

- Course strategy. What we run, what we add, what we drop, and what we charge.
- Both existing courses, end to end. Curriculum, structure, and how they get better each time.
- The AI course, from nothing to a first paying cohort.
- Anything else you think we should be selling. This is the part of the job with no edges.

**The delivery**

- Running four cohorts a year. Enrolment, comms, materials, the sessions, the IRL day, the dinner, the
  feedback afterwards. There's no admin support, so this is yours too.
- The coaches. Managing the three we have, bringing in the ones we need, and holding the standard.
- Course feedback is the best thing we have. Keeping it there while cohorts get bigger is harder than
  it sounds, and it's on you.

**The systems**

- The systems that keep a cohort feeling personally looked after. Courses run on automation across
  Claude, Zapier, HubSpot and Mailchimp. You own all of it, and you'll rebuild the bits that deserve
  rebuilding.
- Finding the work that shouldn't be done by a person. Reporting, cohort trackers, enrolment
  sequences, chasing, updating. If you're doing it for the fourth time by hand, that's the flag. We'd
  rather you spent a day building the thing than a month doing the task.
- Using AI properly, not performatively. We're a team of four doing the work of a much bigger one, and
  that only holds if we're genuinely good at this. You'll be expected to build tools for yourself, and
  to have an opinion on what AI should and shouldn't touch when the product is people. What you learn
  here feeds the AI course you'll be selling.

## How much rope you'll get

Some idea of what "yours to run" has meant here. All of this is the person leaving the role.

- She took The Marketing Accelerator from good to our best performing course. That meant bringing in
  professional coaches, adding an IRL day and the graduation dinner, putting the price up by 150%, and
  running paid ads for the first time. She proposed it, then ran it.
- She rebuilt Brand Manager Boot Camp from the course structure up, including the podcast that now
  sits alongside it.
- She's spent the last few months building AI agents to take the repeatable parts of this job off her
  plate: drafting the launch emails, screening applicants, pulling the reporting together. Some of it
  works and some of it is half built. You'll inherit all of it and where you take it is up to you.
  Nobody asked her to do any of it. She wanted her afternoons back.
- She asked for six weeks off and a shorter week, and got both.

None of that came from a brief. It came from someone deciding it should exist.

## About you

You get a kick out of putting people in a room together and would sooner spend an afternoon building
the automation than do the same task forty times. You like owning something whole, and you'd rather
have one revenue line you're accountable for than a seat at the table on five. You're happy being the
brain in the middle, briefing a designer, a copywriter and a paid specialist rather than doing all of
it yourself, and you're equally happy doing it yourself at 8pm the day before a launch. You can take a
half-formed idea and come back with a plan, a timeline and a view on whether it's worth doing. And
you'd rather look at what the numbers say and change course than defend a decision you've already made.

This is a job with real ownership in it. You're not passing the good bits to someone else, and you're
not being handed a tidy machine to keep ticking over. There isn't anyone else.

- **You've launched a paid product and sold it.** Not contributed to one. Yours, from the idea to the
  first person paying for it. This is the thing we'll dig into hardest.
- **You're comfortable selling.** A good chunk of this job is getting someone curious to pay, on a
  call, in a launch, or across a room, and you don't find that awkward.
- **You can market it as well as build it.** You've written the launch emails, briefed the ads, and
  watched a funnel not work and fixed it.
- **Experience running a programme with a start and an end date,** online and in person. A cohort, a
  season, an event series. You've hosted the event, chased the RSVPs, and fixed the thing that broke
  ten minutes before it started.
- **A systems brain.** You've built automations that other people now rely on, and you're already
  using AI to do parts of your job rather than reading about it.
- **You're happy owning a number,** and honest when it's going the wrong way. You'd tell us in week
  three, not at the end of the quarter.
- **Comfortable moving fast with imperfect information.** This is a small team and progress beats
  polish most weeks.
- **An interest in startup marketing** and the people doing it. You'll be in the room with our members
  constantly.

Tools we use: Claude, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier.

## Success in 3 months

- You've run a cohort launch end to end and you know exactly what worked and what didn't.
- You've delivered a full course cycle, from promotion through to feedback.
- You can tell us why people don't buy, from talking to people who didn't.
- You've got a plan for the next cohort's numbers that you believe in, and we do too.
- The AI course exists as a proposition. Not a finished course, but a shape, a price and a date.
- You've found three things we do by hand that we shouldn't, and killed them.
- You've told us something about our courses we didn't already know.

## Written application

Three questions, 150 words each. No cover letter, we read every one.

1. Go and look at The Marketing Accelerator. Tell us why someone would decide not to buy it.
2. What's a growth experiment you'd love to try for The Marketing Accelerator, and how would you know
   if it worked?
3. What's the coolest thing you've created with AI which has changed how you or your team work?

We use AI all day here, so use it for this if you want. We'd just rather read something you've thought
about than something that sounds like everyone else.

## Video interview

Two questions, two minutes each. Recorded, not live. We're not marking your lighting.

1. Sell us The Marketing Accelerator. You've had twenty minutes on our website and nothing else to go
   on.
2. Tell us one thing you'd change about how Up World looks from the outside. Our site, our newsletter,
   our LinkedIn, one of our courses. Be specific, and don't be polite about it.

## Notes on how this was built (Aug 2026)

- **The constraint is demand, not delivery.** Cohorts run at 20 to 25 against a target of 40, with a
  playbook that already works. So roughly 60% more people per cohort, four times a year, is where the
  doubling comes from. The spec screens for that and treats delivery competence as a hard requirement
  rather than the pitch.
- **"You've launched a paid product and sold it" is the primary screen.** It's the one requirement
  that covers both halves of the job, growing what exists and building what doesn't, and it can't be
  satisfied by someone who has only ever contributed to someone else's launch.
- **Brand Hackers minis and Up Academy are framed as possibilities, not promises,** because neither is
  agreed. Named anyway, because they're most of the reason the job is interesting.
- **No line management.** Nzola reports to the Head of Up World. That makes this a senior individual
  contributor role, which is the one thing that makes the band plausible.
- **Wording carried over from the community manager spec wherever possible,** because that copy was
  signed off by Lottie and needless rewriting buys another review cycle. Kept: the ownership and
  automation paragraphs, the course descriptions, the "tear up something that works" paragraph, most
  of About you, and the reporting line.
- **Standing concern, on the record:** someone who can take a revenue line from £100k to £200k while
  owning strategy, product, delivery and the funnel is a commercial hire, and this band is light for
  that. Not Liv's call, so the spec is written to the band, but Lottie should have heard it.
