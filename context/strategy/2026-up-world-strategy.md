# Up World 2026 Strategy

Source: Google Doc "Up World 2026 Strategy - Shortened," pulled from Drive 2026-07-22.

## Where we're going
Up World is community and curated learning for ambitious marketers — a place where you can admit
you're stuck, and it feels like hanging out with friends, because the audience is in these roles
for passion and purpose.

**By 2030:** 5,000 members who've found their tribe within Up World. Up Club membership seen as
essential to doing a start-up marketing role well — if it's not in your new-role package, you
kick off; if you lose your bank card, you scramble to update payment details. Seen as the heart of
the start-up marketing ecosystem, influential in shaping how marketing teams work.

**Why:** to help more ambitious start-up marketers do better work through community + curated learning.

## Where 2025 left us
Cohorts increased buzz and engagement. Real momentum behind BUC (Big Up Club). People love Up Club
enough to stand in a rainy field for it (Up Fest). Navigated a shifting team structure, found
rhythm again, and — if projections hold — closed the year with a profit.

## The three 2026 pillars

### 1. Be unforgettable — deliver a WOM-worthy experience at every touchpoint
Strong evidence the PD workshop + Marketing Accelerator generate word-of-mouth; the question is
spreading that throughout the funnel.
- **Metrics:** UC churn maintained 4-6%, BUC churn limited to 2-4/month
- **Key tactics:** fix BUC churn via re-engagement strategy (Abbie); an events programme (UC + BUC)
  planned well in advance, IRL events that are never corporate and contain genuine
  surprise/delight (examples: Up Fest postcards, Jo's visualisation exercise at PD workshop that
  got people emotional); review the funnel for a WOM-worthy moment at each step; improve Up Fest
  (speakers, wet-weather plan, experience design); Comm Exec continually tracks member feedback,
  incl. the cancellation process.

### 2. Drive a step change in the Up World brand
Not currently confident the company is telling members' stories, leading the marketing
conversation, or proud of all its content-channel output — a real risk to the "best place for
ambitious marketers to grow" positioning.
- **Metrics:** near-zero instances of spotting poor-quality content; strong social/newsletter
  engagement; people leave Up World buzzing
- **Key tactics:** clear jump in Socials + email quality/engagement (build on Kasturi + Robin's
  work, likely hire a Brand Manager); clearer metrics on brand impact; get brilliant at publicly
  sharing member success stories; own the marketing conversation and track trends; make sure the
  website reflects the brand.

### 3. Financial stability — not crazy growth, stay in the green
Not building a cash cow or aiming for rocket-ship growth. Modest membership growth + a refreshed
sponsorship programme funds a potential Brand Manager hire and reinvestment into member experience.
- **Metrics:** £50k profit, 10-15% margin; hit revenue-driver targets for cohorts, sponsorship, and courses
- **Key tactics:** always-on marketing + growth tactics to reach UC cohorts of 100/150/150; small
  BUC membership increase (cohorts of 20/20/20); take sponsorship to a level similar to Generalist
  World; right team in place (great CX onboarding, Alfie replacement, smooth Liv cover); fix
  reporting so UC/BUC actuals are quick to calculate and forecasts map onto actuals; run 2
  Marketing Accelerators.

## Watch-outs (overall company risk)
- **Are we always working on the most important things?** Biggest expense is salary/team time.
  Past pattern: everyone trying to work on everything. Need to build the delegation muscle,
  especially with the added Comm Exec resource. Warning signs: "I'm spending too much time on this
  manual task," "I'm not getting to my topline priority because of lots of smaller tasks."
- **H1 priority: having each other's backs while Liv is away.** Comparable to how the team handled
  Hope and Rachel leaving. Liv needs to set the team up for success; Abbie and Jonny need to be on
  top of it.
