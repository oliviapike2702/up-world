# 2026 Strategy — companion notes

Source: Google Doc "2026 Strategy summary" (a ChatGPT summary of a separate strategy
conversation), pulled from Drive 2026-07-22. Distinct from, but overlapping with,
`2026-up-world-strategy.md` — treat as a companion angle, not a duplicate. Links to a "Rough
notes" doc not yet opened.

## Vision & positioning
- Purpose: help ambitious start-up marketers do better work through community + curated learning
- Shift further toward *learning* as the core value proposition
- Aspiration: be *the* place for start-up marketing — very cool, aspirational, exceptional
  quality; win awards; lead conversations on marketing careers; define "The Up World Way"

## Content & thought leadership
- Focus on exclusive insight from top-tier marketers, not Googleable stuff
- Establish "what is great start-up marketing?" as a core theme
- Formats: Up Talks + workshops (strategy + personal development), LinkedIn-shareable
  certificates, a Lottie-led podcast (potential future business unit), "Member Mondays" and
  "romanticise going to events" style social content
- Action needed: get clear on what "curated learning" means in practice

## Community growth & targeting
- Target audience: ambitious start-up marketers, strongest today in FMCG, also fashion, beauty,
  pet care, sports
- Next 500 members: be intentional, segment by sector and channel
- Goal: 200 members per cohort
- Acquisition levers: lead-gen dinners (6 people, targeted), event partnerships (e.g. Klaviyo,
  agencies), presence at MADFest/Festival of Marketing/Marketing Meetup, invite-only pizza nights,
  "golden ticket" events for non-members, ambassadors + partner brands

## Member experience & retention
- Priorities: faster answers, practical help, curated learning; improve the Slack experience with
  the 2026 exec; turn members into champions/raving superfans
- Engagement: intimate spaces within big forums so people aren't lost in Slack; curated career
  development (PD workshops, CV clinics); more certificates, clearer progression
- Roles: Community Exec owns retention/engagement; Brand Manager bridges the brand-curation gap

## Growth & marketing
- Priorities: clarify brand comms/growth strategy, stronger LinkedIn hooks, sector-specific
  "Season of Up Talks," PDF playbooks with mentors + target lists, experiment with member/prospect
  mix at events
- Tactics: cold email campaign, partner with agencies for event buzz, promote "curated learning"
  across channels, "come with me" storytelling on social

## Operations & execution
- One workshop a month (PD or marketing strategy) — kept easy to run, no snacks, just pizza + drinks
- Test a monthly Klaviyo coworking day (Fridays)
- Freelancers for social support, possibly a paid role
- Internal actions: Abbie to delegate more; fix socials (Laura); hire/repurpose someone for
  social + written content; plan cohort timings + segmentation; chase granular tracking and metrics

## Key 2026 goals (as stated in this doc)
1. Strengthen curated learning as Up World's unique value
2. Define and own "The Up World Way"
3. Be known as the aspirational home for start-up marketers
