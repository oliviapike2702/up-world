# Up World brand guidelines

Harvested from "Up World Brand Guide V1" (the brand book PDF in Drive, dated May 2025) and
"UP WORLD COLOURS.pdf". This file is the text version an agent can actually use; the PDFs
remain the visual reference — see `asset-map.md` for links.

## The brand in one line
A friendly space for ambitious marketers who want to achieve big things and help others do
the same. Founded by Lottie Unwin after her own "thrilling but brutally lonely" crash landing
into start-up marketing.

## Brand house
- **North Star:** Thriving together
- **Archetype:** Backpackers, with a shared desire to keep learning and experience
  alternative points of view
- **Brand DNA:** Kudos · 'Feel at home' · Experts

**For:** the journey, not the end goal.
**With:** an entrepreneurial community with kudos, beta-mode mindset, next-level exposés,
hugs not handshakes, alternative perspectives, expert hacks and case studies, steal with
pride to make it your own.
**Against:** silver bullets, going it alone, info overload, one size fits all.

**Words members use about us:** connected, inspired, supported, eager, excited, hungry,
motivated, informed.

## Colours
| Colour | Hex | CMYK | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up World Pink | `#E7536E` | C3 M83 Y42 K0 | Primary |
| Up World Navy | `#16173D` | C97 M93 Y43 K52 | Primary |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | — | Complement |

- Pink and navy are used together at roughly **80/20** — one dominant, one accent (the
  accent highlights key information or callouts).
- Either colour can be the dominant one; they are interchangeable.

## Typography
Primary typeface is **Figtree** (a free Google Font), in three weights only:
- **Figtree Black** — main headings, or to highlight key text within longer passages
- **Figtree Bold** — subheadings, isolated callouts
- **Figtree Regular** — all body copy

## Logos
Two logo families: **Up World** and **Up Club**, each available in pink and navy, plus
Up World roundels (three versions) and a newsletter logo. Files live in the Drive `Logos`
folder (see `asset-map.md`).

⚠ Only PNGs are on Drive — no vector/SVG masters found. If print or large-format is ever
needed, chase down the source files.

## Illustration style
- A **"wonky" hand-drawn illustration style** is the distinctive Up World visual personality —
  used throughout communications to add friendliness and charm.
- **Hand-drawn underlines** may be used sparingly to underline key words or phrases, always
  in the opposing colour to the background (navy background → pink lines, and vice versa).
- A set of **holding shapes** is used for headings, quotes and callouts.

## Photography
- Brand photography is a window into our environment and the **diversity of people we
  support** — real community, not stock.
- Imagery may sit behind text or graphics as long as the text is clearly legible; apply a
  darker overlay when needed for standout.
- The **wonky circle** can hold imagery, always with a **white keyline**.
- Real photos live on Drive: `Community Pics` and `Photos of Lottie` (see `asset-map.md`).

## Personality and voice (summary only)
The brand book defines the voice as **"your infectious mate"** with three TOV pillars:
kind connectors · straight up · smart, with a smile. Not brash, not cutesy.

For actual writing, **the operational source of truth is `../tov/`** (`_spine.md` + the
voice docs), which is more current and more detailed than the brand book's TOV pages.

## Audience (summary only)
The brand book defines two design-target personas: **Charlotte** (DTC marketing manager,
25–35) and **Dean** (Head of Marketing/CMO, 35–45), with freelancers, founders and service
providers as secondary. Fuller and more current audience research lives in `../audience/`.
