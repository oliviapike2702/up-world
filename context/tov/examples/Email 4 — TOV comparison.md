### What's wrong with it

- Opens with a limp statement rather than a story or provocation
- Testimonials are dropped in with no context or framing — feels like a quote dump
- "Some context from previous cohorts" is corporate phrasing, nobody talks like that
- "If you've been sitting on it" is the only interesting line and it's buried at the end
- No Lottie voice — could be from any course, any brand

### What changed and why

- **Opener:** "A few people have replied asking..." is warmer and more specific than "a few people have asked" — it implies the emails are working, people are engaging
- **"Fair question. I'd ask the same."** — self-aware, disarming, Lottie
- **Framing paragraph before the quotes** — sets up what the course does in Lottie's voice before dumping testimonials. The reader understands the range of outcomes first
- **Gabby's quote is introduced, not just dropped** — "She put it like this:" gives it weight
- **"That's a promotion. That's what I built this for."** — lands the quote. Punchy. Tells the reader how significant that is without over-explaining
- **Placeholders remain** but are framed more specifically (skill/confidence shift, punchy outcome) so whoever fills them knows what to look for
- **No em dashes, no "some context from", no corporate framing**
