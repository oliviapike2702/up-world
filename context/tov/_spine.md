# Voice spine — hard rules shared by ALL voices
✓ Migrate from existing "Up World tone of voice.md" + "Anti AI Writing.rtf".

- UK English. **No em dashes** (the biggest AI tell).
- No fabricated facts, numbers, names, or quotes.
- Be specific: real numbers, real brands, real names.
- One-person "you". Sentence-case headings.
- No hype without backing. Ban: delve, leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, elevate, curated,
  bespoke, showcase, moreover, furthermore (see full list in migrated TOV).
- Ban structures: "not just X, it's Y", rule-of-three, "-ing" analysis tags, vague sourcing.
