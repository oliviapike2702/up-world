## Course structure

This course is 12 weeks long, covering 8 modules:

- Module 1 — What is a Brand (and Who Are You Talking To?)
- Module 2 — Brand Strategy
- Module 3 — Marketing Strategy and Planning
- Module 4 — Ways of Working
- Module 5 — Briefing and Creative
- Module 6 — Marketing Channels
- Module 7 — Your Career
- Module 8 — Measurement & What Comes Next

Each module pairs a lecture with either a **Group Case Study Workshop** or a **Brand Hackers Technical Deep Dive** (CRM, Socials, Paid Ads, Trade Marketing — modules 1, 3, 5 and 7).

**Lectures**: an hour long over Zoom, delivered by Lottie. We really recommend making sure you can make it to these — it's always easier said than done to catch up afterwards! There's room for questions but fair warning, it is an hour of information.

**Workshops and technical deep dives**: the week after each lecture, either a group case study session or a Brand Hackers clinic on that module's technical topic.
