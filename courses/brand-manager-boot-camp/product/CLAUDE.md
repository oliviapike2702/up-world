## Core problem BMBC solves
Big companies (P&G, Unilever, L'Oréal) invest heavily in training their marketers. Start-ups don't. They hire someone and hope. Most startup brand managers are figuring out how to do the job as they go — they have the intelligence and drive but nobody ever gave them the framework. BMBC is that framework.

## Voice and tone rules
- UK English (colour, organise, analyse, licence)
- No em dashes. Use commas, brackets, or a new sentence.
- No emojis
- Lead with the answer or the story. No preamble.
- Plain-spoken, direct. Short words over long. Cut anything that can go without losing meaning.
- Contractions fine. No hedging stacks.
- Prose by default. Bullets only when they carry real structure.
- No rule-of-three constructions.
- No contrast structures ("not X but Y").
- No signposting lines.
- **Banned words/phrases:** delve, tapestry, leverage, unlock, superpower, in conclusion, it's worth noting, "great question", "I'd be happy to", "here's the thing"
- Story-led. Each email has one story or observation at its centre. The sell comes last and briefly.
