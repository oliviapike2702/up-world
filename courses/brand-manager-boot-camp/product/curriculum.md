# BMBC 2026 — curriculum

Source: live site copy at https://www.up-world.co/brand-manager-bootcamp (confirmed current by Liv, 2026-08-04). Pairing of each lecture with its Technical Deep Dive / Group Case Study Workshop per `CLAUDE.md`.

## Module 1: What is a Brand (and Who Are You Talking To?)
Paired with: Technical Deep Dive — CRM
- What a brand actually is and what it isn't
- What brand management involves day to day (it's more than you think and different to what you'd expect)
- The difference between data, observation and insight
- How real consumer insight drives everything that comes after & how to get it

## Module 2: Brand Strategy
Paired with: Group Case Study Workshop
- What brand strategy is, in plain English
- Brand positioning: how to find where you belong and why it matters
- How to write a one-page brand strategy you could actually use
- How to connect your brand work to business outcomes without needing a finance degree
- How to talk about strategy to people who don't care about strategy

## Module 3: Marketing Strategy and Planning
Paired with: Technical Deep Dive — Socials
- How strategy becomes a plan: turning insight into action
- How to build an activity plan that's actually deliverable
- Prioritisation when everything feels urgent
- Managing a project without a project manager

## Module 4: Ways of Working
Paired with: Group Case Study Workshop
- Managing up: setting expectations, giving updates, flagging problems before they become fires
- How to present your work and get decisions made
- Communication style and reading the room
- What good looks like as a junior in a marketing team

## Module 5: Briefing and Creative
Paired with: Technical Deep Dive — Paid Ads
- How to write a brief that actually helps creative teams do their best work
- How to judge creative work without defaulting to personal taste
- How to give feedback that's useful rather than just critical
- What agencies or freelancers need from clients that most clients never give them

## Module 6: Marketing Channels
Paired with: Group Case Study Workshop
- How to think about channel selection: reach, role and budget
- OOH: how it works, when to use it and how to buy it
- Meta ads: how to brief performance teams and know if it's working
- Sampling: when getting the product in someone's hands beats anything digital
- How the channels work together rather than against each other

## Module 7: Your Career
Paired with: Technical Deep Dive — Trade Marketing
- What the brand career path actually looks like: the roles, the jumps, the timelines
- How to build your own personal development plan
- Identifying your strengths and where to put your energy
- How to get something useful out of feedback
- How to think about the kind of marketer you're becoming, not just the next job title

## Module 8: Measurement & What Comes Next
Paired with: Group Case Study Workshop
- How to set KPIs that connect to what you're actually trying to do
- How to analyse what worked and why, not just report on what happened
- How to present performance in a way that drives a decision
- How the 8 weeks connect as a system
- The habits and behaviours that separate good brand managers from great ones
