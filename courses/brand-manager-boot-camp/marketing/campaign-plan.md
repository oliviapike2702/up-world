# BMBC Sept 2026 — email campaign plan (working draft)

The runway from now to kickoff. **Not final** — a shared vision we sharpen as we go. Drafted
14 Jul 2026 using the `../../email-playbook.md` and the results log. Each email is drafted fresh
in its week (never pre-baked), reading the results log + playbook first. Visual version: Artifact
(link in chat).

- **Goal:** fill the 12 remaining spots (8 of 20 sold, early bird closed).
- **Kickoff:** 3 September 2026. **Price:** £999 (early bird gone).
- **Cadence:** heavier — roughly weekly, clustered around the webinar, with a final-week double.
- **Pillar:** the 4 Aug webinar, "How to be a great brand manager" (Lottie fireside with Naomi +
  Efua), sent by Nzola in the Up World voice. Everything bends around it.
- **Lever:** scarcity of places, not an early-bird deadline (that's dead). Per TMA retro: places
  are applied for, not bought.
- **Voice:** Lottie for the sales emails; Up World "we" voice (Nzola) for the webinar emails.

## How each email gets made
1. In its week, read the playbook + results log + this plan + the campaign facts.
2. Draft in the right voice. Never send — drafts only, Liv approves.
3. Liv checks four things: TOV, key info, look, dates/hard facts.
4. Capture any correction back into the playbook (§7 failure log grows).
5. After send: log clicks/unsubs/sign-ups; note one thing to change next time.
