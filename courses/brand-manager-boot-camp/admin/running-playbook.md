# BMBC running playbook — Nzola

Nzola's guide to running the course once people have paid: everything from pre-kick-off setup
through to the grad dinner. Two parts: get everything live before 3 Sept, then keep it running
week to week. Source: 2026 BMBC planning tracker + Liv's brief, 11 Aug 2026.

## Running the course, week to week

BMBC is 12 weeks, 8 live modules (see `../product/curriculum.md`). Content is being written as
the course goes this cohort rather than pre-loaded, so most of the below happens live, week by
week, not batched up front.

**Coaches**
Before each session: confirm the coach is confident with the material and knows how to log on to
Zoom. Don't assume — check with them directly.

**Soft skills workshops**
Capacity target per session: minimum 3, maximum 4 signed up. Keep a running list of who's marked
against which slot. If someone isn't showing up to a session they signed up to, that's Nzola's to
catch and chase, not something to notice after the fact.

**Brand Hackers (technical deep dives — modules 1, 3, 5, 7)**
Ahead of each one: send the slide template, check in on whether they need any help prepping.

**Recordings**
Upload every session recording to the drive after it happens.

**Lottie's decks and cribb sheets**
Lottie's making these in batches of 2. Give her 2 weeks' notice before each batch is due — keep
a running "what's due when" so nothing lands on her with no runway.

**Prep documents**
Any pre-work needed before a workshop gets sent out to the group ahead of time, every time.

**Slack and email comms**
Scheduled week by week rather than all at once (a change from how this usually runs, because
content's being finished as the course goes). Zoom links, homework, feedback asks — whatever's
due that week goes out on its own cadence.

**Feedback**
Collect throughout the course. Review and feed back every couple of weeks, not after every
single session.

**Testimonials**
Collect as they come in through the course, don't leave it to the end.

## Grad dinner
Confirm exact numbers coming, order any merch/book in good time, keep it special — this is the
one bit of BMBC that's IRL and memorable, treat it that way.
