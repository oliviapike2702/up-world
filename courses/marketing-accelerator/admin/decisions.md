# TMA — decisions, rationale, open items

Source: Handover Jan 2026 doc. This is the "why," not the "what" — pairs with `systems.md`
(the how) and `product/tma-product-facts.md` (current-state facts).

## Decisions already made, with rationale
- **Price raised by £500** when the IRL kick-off day was added. Before the IRL day, attendance
  dropped to ~60% by course end; since adding it, attendance has held ~90%+ throughout. The IRL
  day is the reason for the increase, not general inflation.
- **Course platform moved from Thinkific to plain Google Drive.** Per the doc: "worked GREAT and
  didn't cost us anything."
- **Interview process (15-min phone/Zoom calls) introduced March 2025** — run by Lottie or Olivia
  + Marc organising. Applicants "want to be listened to," often open up emotionally in these
  calls; knowing the course content well is described as "pretty vital."
