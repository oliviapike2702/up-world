# The Marketing Accelerator (TMA) — product facts

Harvested 8 July 2026 from the Handover doc (Jan 2026), the Accelerator Jan 2026 planning sheet, and live HubSpot data. Facts are as of the March 2026 cohort; update per cohort. Conflicts flagged inline.

## What it is
A 12-week programme "meticulously designed to grow your leadership skills, marketing ability and ultimately to accelerate your career." Runs twice a year (March and September starts). 6 modules; each module = 1 x 1-hour Zoom lecture, then 1 x group coaching session the following week, plus a podcast episode and a crib sheet. No homework. One bonus lecture ("Getting to know your customer") with no coaching session. Course is hosted on Google Drive (moved off Thinkific; free and works well).

## Two promises
1. Transforms the effectiveness of your work.
2. Builds the confidence and mindset to transition into being a leader.

## Audience
Marketing Managers / Senior Marketing Managers a couple of years in, aiming for Head of Marketing but needing leadership skills; also new or returning Heads of Marketing needing a confidence boost (e.g. post mat leave). Mostly women, mostly D2C/start-up marketers. Qualification by job title: HOT = MM/Senior MM; too senior = Marketing Director/Founder; too junior = Assistant/Executive/Intern.

Internal-only positioning candour (never external): "At the end of this course half of them will quit their jobs! We want their companies to pay so we OBVS don't say this to anyone."
