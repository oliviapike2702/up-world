## As actually sent in 2026 (vs the skeleton below)
12 Mailchimp sends, 9 Jan to 5 Mar 2026, launch email "Ready for round seven?" (14 Jan). Skipped from the planned skeleton: Accelerator vs Ritson, standalone grad stories, podcast reveal, and the "last day/last week" pair; no Dec hot-leads sends in Mailchimp (those went via HubSpot). ## Mailchimp segments
- Accelerator 2026 List engaged (~3,700): 30-day engaged, minus flows/bounced/never-engaged/grads/not-relevant. Gets ALL accelerator comms.
- Accelerator 2026 List All (~4,750): 90-day engaged + often/sometimes engagement. Bigger announcements only.
- All audience: whole database minus excludes (used for the open-to-all talk).
- Seniors / Mid Levels: the "excludes grads and course opt-outs" list split by seniority.
