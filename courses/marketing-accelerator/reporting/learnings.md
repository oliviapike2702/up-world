# TMA March 2026 — what worked, what hurt (from Slack, as it happened)

Harvested from #start-up-accelerator and #startup-accelerator-team, Nov 2025 to Apr 2026. Note: #the-marketing-accelerator channel is dormant (last real activity Jan 2025); the launch ran in the other two.

## What worked (keep and systematise)
- **Slick systems + demand.** Lottie, 19 Feb: "IT'S REALLY WORKING!! ... so much easier than previous years with 1) slick systems and 2) great market demand." Marc on the new HubSpot process: "8 booked in, taking me almost no time."
- **Lottie doing all interviews** (from early Feb, gated to Wednesday PMs) lifted conversion; 7 to 8 calls on peak days.
- **Automated Calendly email on the 'arranging interview' stage change** plus a new 'more info needed' stage with auto email + 7-day follow-up: fixed the booking leak mid-launch.
- **Lottie's UGC ad video**: 5 of 9 direct applications in its first week. Direct ads CPL fell £95 → ~£31 once conversion tracking was fixed.
- **Abbie cold-calling the 15 unresponsives** (13 Mar): recovered call-backs and flushed out pull-outs before the deadline.
- **Deadline mechanics**: external 6 Mar, internal 13 Mar, then hustle-to-the-last-minute; sales closed late as always, ads extended to the external deadline with scarcity creative.
- **Payment-plan contracts** (Hana, Nov): fixed the "stopped paying halfway with no obligation" problem from the previous cohort.

## The ten pain points (playbook must design these out)
1. **Interview booking was the biggest funnel leak.** Applications flowed; bookings didn't. Lottie's end state: "as soon as someone applies we call them immediately and interview then and there... two people today had applied a few weeks ago and couldn't remember what it was about; the moment had passed."
2. **Stale assets leaked into live comms all launch long**: "waitlist for Jan 2026" CTA in December, £1,299.99 old pricing live in a HubSpot email in March, broken Shopify early-bird button, broken website links flagged three separate times. Playbook needs a pre-launch asset audit checklist with an owner.
3. **HubSpot automation bugs firefought mid-flight**: everyone getting the partial-applicant email; paid people still receiving payment reminders; deals arriving with no value; emails not logged ("impossible to see what stage anyone is at", Lottie). Needs a pre-launch workflow test pass with dummy applicants.
4. **Post-interview payment chasing was heavy and ad hoc.** Verbal confirms sat unpaid for weeks. Lottie, 26 Feb: "plan a calendar of comms for the post-interview funnel so it's all ready to go with less manual input, e.g. 'XX% of places are confirmed now'." This is a job for the email agent.
5. **Duplicated tracking**: sheet vs HubSpot conflict, manual ad attribution, emoji reactions used as pipeline reporting. Pick one source of truth per launch.
6. **Quiz ads produce volume, not applications.** No application from quiz leads after 26 Jan; the follow-up email that converts them had no owner.
7. **Brand discipline drifted under sales pressure.** Lottie, 13 Feb: places are applied for, not bought; one chaser "feels a bit desperate"; early bird is the wrong urgency when companies pay, scarcity of awarded places is the right one. Voice guardrails belong in the email agent's context.
8. **Tool access friction burned the first weeks**: Meta 2FA codes over Slack, community phone abandoned for personal WhatsApp, Mailchimp sender setup, missing 2024 grads list.
9. **Interview logistics noise**: Google Meet links confusing phone interviewees (deleted manually every invite), no-shows, manual diary sweeps.
10. **Course-running snags with launch roots**: coaching capacity maths done after kick-off (20 slots for 22 people, +£600 extra session), Luma caps edited one by one, feedback forms ignored until individually chased, coach recordings turned off.
