## Findings worth keeping

**1. The master template's APPLY NOW buttons ship with `href="#"` by default.** This is a documented limitation (`context/how-to/mailchimp.md`), not something this session broke: `mc:edit` regions let the API fill button text but never the href. Email 3's better-written draft had this exact problem, all three buttons went nowhere. Fixed by rebuilding it as a plain custom-HTML campaign (real link, but loses click-to-edit on wording, same trade Liv's team already made for the sent launch email). Worth checking any future template-built draft for this before it gets close to sending.

Two stale drafts are sitting under email 3 and email 4's slots in Mailchimp, unfiled.** Both look like earlier, unfinished attempts to adapt the March cohort's raw copy (early bird pricing, Sam Thomson named, Jonny as sender) rather than the current calendar brief.
