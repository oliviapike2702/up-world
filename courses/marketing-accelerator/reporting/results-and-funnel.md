## HubSpot pipeline reality (deals created Nov 2025 to Apr 2026, all 222 read)
By month created: Dec 4 · Jan 103 · Feb 76 · Mar 17. Final stages:
Partial Applied 95 · Not right 32 · Paid 23 · More info needed 21 · Decided to pull out 17 · Unresponsive 15 · Acceptance sent 6 · Declined too junior 3 · Interested in next cohort 2 · WA chaser sent 2 · WA+LI chaser sent 2 · Said they'll pay 1.

Reading of it:
- 43% of all deals died at Partial Applied (started the form, never finished). Biggest single leak; there is an auto-email + Lottie LinkedIn DM, but it is worth a proper chaser sequence.
- ~10% application-to-paid overall.
- "Decided to pull out" (17) + "Interested in next cohort" (2) are the first warm audience for the September 2026 launch.
- Duplicate person deals exist (form resubmissions create new deals); data hygiene item.
- "Enrolled" stage is unused in practice; Paid is the de facto terminal stage.

Meta ads: Alfie ran Jan 2025 (£4,168 spend, ROAS ~2); Adelle (Brand Hackers) took over from 16 Dec 2025 for the 2026 launch. 2026 ads: quiz completions ~50 to 99/week at £3 to £4.50 per quiz but near-zero quiz-to-application conversion after late Jan; direct-campaign CPL fell from £95 to ~£31 to £37 once fixed; Lottie's UGC video drove 5 of 9 direct applications in its first week. Adelle's verdict for next time: ~£1K on quiz (data/retargeting only), rest on direct, UGC ready before going live. Volume engine, weak converter. Email and referral are the quality engines.
