# Courses — strategy and targets (Sept 2026)

Source: "Replacement job spec" session, drafting the Courses Lead / Product Marketing Manager
hire. Captures the bigger-picture decisions behind that hire, not just the role spec itself
(see `context/hiring/product-marketing-manager-task.md` for the task/person spec).

## Current state
- Revenue: ~£100k/year across 4 cohorts a year (2x Brand Manager Boot Camp + 2x The Marketing
  Accelerator), each cohort running 20-25 people.
- Ownership: moving to a single Courses Lead / PMM hire who owns BMBC + TMA end-to-end
  (product, marketing, delivery, admin) solo, with no dedicated admin support. Nzola has no
  course responsibility from Sept 2026 (see `context/team.md`).

## Target
- Double revenue to ~£200k/year within 2 years, primarily by growing cohort size (20-25 → 40
  people per cohort), not by adding more cohorts per year.

## What's agreed vs. just an idea
- **Agreed**: the differentiators to keep leaning on are the **group/personal development
  coaching sessions** and the **graduation dinner** — these are what the courses already win on.
- **Agreed, dated**: a new AI course is targeted for **start of 2027**.
- **Idea only, not agreed**: Brand Hackers-style mini-courses (CRM, social, paid) and a
  speculative "Up Academy" concept. Don't treat either as roadmapped until Lottie/Liv confirm.
